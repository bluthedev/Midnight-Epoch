# Midnight Epoch: A Horologist's Tale

![Midnight Epoch](screenshot.png)

**Midnight Epoch** is a premium, skill-based 2D side-scrolling stealth action game built natively for the TON ecosystem via Telegram Mini Apps. Moving away from standard tap-to-earn mechanics, it delivers fluid, physics-driven parkour, tactical stealth, and immersive lore based around high-end horology and techwear. 

It aims to onboard Web2 gamers to Web3 seamlessly through engaging gameplay and true digital asset ownership.

---

## The World of Aether-7
The game takes place in Aether-7, a hyper-capitalist, vertically-stacked megacity. It is strictly divided:
- **The Citadel:** The pristine, gold-and-white brutalist structures of the upper levels, controlled by a centralized tech monopoly.
- **The Underground:** A labyrinth of neon-lit, gritty industrial zones where the decentralized resistance thrives.

## The Horologists
Players do not control traditional assassins. They play as "Horologists" — elite operatives belonging to a decentralized syndicate obsessed with precision, timing, and flawless execution. 

Their primary tool is a bespoke, mechanical luxury timepiece that serves as a grappling hook, a hacking interface, and houses a retractable monomolecular wire for silent takedowns.

---

## Core Gameplay Features

- **Fluid Parkour:** A completely custom, decoupled state machine allowing players to run, jump, wall-bounce, slide, and dash through the air with zero friction and maximum flow.
- **Tactical Stealth & Combat:** Utilize shadows and vents to avoid Citadel guard vision cones, executing silent takedowns. Engage in timing-based sword combat when stealth fails.
- **Dynamic Missions:** Gameplay involves 'Data Heists' (extracting encrypted ledgers), Assassinations, and auto-scrolling speedrun Chases.

## Web3 & Telegram Integration

Blockchain elements are woven directly into the lore and mechanics without friction:
- **TON Connect:** Seamless, in-game wallet connection overlying the game canvas.
- **NFT Timepieces & Techwear:** Players mint cosmetic and functional assets on the TON blockchain. Different luxury timepieces alter combat stats or dash distances, while bespoke jackets leave custom dash trails.
- **Social Virality:** Integrated Telegram leaderboards, squad ("Syndicate") creation for group district-control, and referral-based reward systems.

## Tech Stack
- **Engine:** [Phaser 4](https://phaser.io)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Bundler:** Vite
- **Blockchain UI:** `@tonconnect/ui`
- **Platform Target:** Telegram Web Apps API
